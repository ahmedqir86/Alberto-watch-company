HERE TO HELP

Care that lasts.

Guidance for choosing, wearing and caring for your timepiece.

How should I care for my watch?+

Wipe with a soft dry cloth, avoid unnecessary impacts, and follow your model's manufacturer guidance for water exposure and servicing.

Do all watches have the same water resistance?+

No. Water-resistance ratings vary by model. Always check the manufacturer's specification before swimming or showering.

How can I request a repair or appraisal?+

Use the contact form to describe your watch and the service you need. This demo does not transmit messages to a live service desk.

Are the prices and products available for purchase?+

Prices shown are illustrative values for this student eProject, not verified ALBERTO retail prices. This educational website does not process purchases or payments.
